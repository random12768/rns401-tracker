Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide lists 'OCD traits' for anal-retentive fixation; the traits described (perfectionism, orderliness, stinginess) correspond to obsessive-compulsive personality traits rather than OCD.
  • The guide lists hysterical personality and sexual dysfunction as phallic fixation outcomes; these are not standard First Aid associations, and phallic fixation is more commonly described as problems with sexual identity and authority.
