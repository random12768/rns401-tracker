Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide classifies severity by IQ range with adaptive functioning as secondary; DSM-5-TR specifies severity by adaptive functioning (conceptual, social, practical domains), with IQ ranges retained only as a rough guide. Questions here use adaptive functioning as the deciding factor.
  • The guide calls fragile X 'X-linked dominant with reduced penetrance'; it is commonly described as X-linked with variable expressivity and anticipation (CGG repeat in FMR1, hypermethylation and silencing), and males are usually more severely affected.
  • The guide lists the repetitive-behavior row for intellectual disability as 'Absent'; stereotypic movements are in fact common in ID, so absence of stereotypies should not be used as a discriminating feature.
