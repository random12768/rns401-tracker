Negative and cognitive symptoms of schizophrenia

Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide says atypical antipsychotics have 'relatively better' efficacy for negative symptoms; current evidence shows only modest, inconsistent benefit and much of it reflects less secondary negative symptoms from EPS, so this should not be overstated.
  • Guide groups D2 blockade as 'harmful' in the mesocortical pathway; the mesocortical dopamine deficit is a hypothesis for negative/cognitive symptoms and typical antipsychotics are not proven to worsen primary negative symptoms, though they can cause secondary ones.
