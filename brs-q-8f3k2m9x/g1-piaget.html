Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide says absent object permanence by 9 months is a global delay/ASD red flag; object permanence normally emerges at about 8-12 months, so absence at 7-9 months is normal.
  • The death-understanding table labels ages 5-9 'early concrete operational', but concrete operations begin around age 7; the guide's own stage table gives 7-11.
