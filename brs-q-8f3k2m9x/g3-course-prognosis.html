Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide states lifetime suicide is about 10% in schizophrenia; recent meta-analyses estimate about 5% lifetime completed suicide (higher near first episode), so 10% is an older, high estimate.
  • Guide gives lifetime prevalence as 1.5% ('corrected from 1%'); most sources including DSM-5-TR place lifetime prevalence at about 0.3 to 1%, so 1.5% appears overstated.
  • Guide states that both parents affected gives about 40% risk and MZ concordance about 50%; these are commonly quoted Step 1 values but vary between studies (MZ concordance 40 to 50%).
