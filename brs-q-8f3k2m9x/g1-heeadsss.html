Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide lists "pregnant" minors as emancipated; pregnancy alone does not emancipate a minor (it permits consent to pregnancy-related care in most states). Emancipation is by court order, marriage, military service, or financial independence, and varies by state.
  • The guide states minors can consent to mental health treatment "generally"; this varies widely by state and age threshold, so the rule should be presented as state-dependent.
  • The guide describes suicide as the 2nd/3rd leading cause of death in adolescents; this is current (unintentional injury is first), but rank varies by age band and year.
