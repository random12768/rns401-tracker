Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide lists Capgras, Fregoli, Cotard, and nihilistic delusions as topics in the scope but its delusion cards omit them; content for these was taken from standard Step 1 resources.
  • Guide labels delusional infestation as an Ekbom syndrome; Ekbom syndrome is also a name for restless legs syndrome, so 'delusional parasitosis' is the clearer term.
  • Guide lists folie a deux under delusional disorder subtypes; DSM-5-TR no longer has a separate shared psychotic disorder and classifies it under other specified schizophrenia spectrum disorder, although it remains a high-yield concept.
