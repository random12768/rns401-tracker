Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide says clozapine requires an REMS program; the FDA removed the clozapine REMS requirement in 2025, although absolute neutrophil count monitoring remains recommended in the prescribing information.
  • The guide calls ziprasidone the atypical with the lowest weight gain; aripiprazole, lurasidone, and ziprasidone all have low metabolic risk, with aripiprazole usually cited as the lowest.
  • The guide does not describe long-acting injectables in detail; the questions use aripiprazole's monthly depot as an example.
