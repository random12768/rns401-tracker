Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide states antidepressant monotherapy is 'contraindicated' in bipolar disorder; it is generally avoided, particularly in bipolar I, but the DSM-5-TR itself counts a full manic syndrome emerging during antidepressant treatment and persisting beyond the drug's physiological effect as a bipolar diagnosis rather than a substance-induced one.
  • The guide says psychosis 'never' occurs in hypomania; this is correct by definition, because any psychotic feature makes the episode manic by DSM criteria.
  • The guide lists valproate for acute mania with the lithium and antipsychotic options; current guidelines also make second-generation antipsychotics (for example olanzapine, quetiapine, risperidone) first-line for acute mania, which the guide mentions only briefly.
