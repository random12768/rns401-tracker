Rett syndrome vs autism vs intellectual disability vs reactive attachment disorder

Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide states Rett syndrome affects 'exclusively' females and is lethal in males; MECP2 variants can survive in males with 47,XXY or somatic mosaicism, and others have severe neonatal encephalopathy. 'Almost exclusively' is more accurate.
  • Rett syndrome was removed as a specifier from the DSM-5 autism spectrum disorder criteria and is not a DSM-5-TR diagnosis; the guide's statement that it 'falls under ASD if criteria met' is outdated, although autistic features during the regression stage are real.
  • The guide lists repetitive behaviors as 'Absent' in intellectual disability; stereotypic movements are common in ID, so this is not a reliable discriminator. Its table also says RAD IQ is normal only 'if history corrected', which is a simplification.
  • Rett disorder is X-linked dominant in most references, but the guide's mnemonic that it is 'exclusively' female should not be used as an absolute rule.
