Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide treats reduce/switch antipsychotic as the first step; current APA guidance recommends VMAT2 inhibitors for moderate to severe or disabling tardive dyskinesia, usually while continuing a needed antipsychotic.
  • The guide states clozapine has no tardive dyskinesia; the risk is very low rather than zero.
