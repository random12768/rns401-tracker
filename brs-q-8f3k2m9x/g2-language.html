Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide gives conflicting thresholds for two-word phrases (24 months in the milestones table, 30 months in the red-flag list); the standard expectation is two-word combinations by 24 months.
  • Guide describes language disorder as an impaired 'speech mechanism'; language disorder is a deficit in comprehension/production of language (vocabulary, grammar, discourse), distinct from speech sound disorder (articulation) and fluency disorder.
  • Guide says audiology is 'always' first; this is standard, but autism screening (M-CHAT-R/F at 18 and 24 months) is also done at routine visits and should not be delayed.
