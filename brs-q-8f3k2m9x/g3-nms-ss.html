Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide compares only neuroleptic malignant syndrome with serotonin syndrome and does not cover malignant hyperthermia or anticholinergic toxicity.
  • The guide lists dantrolene plus bromocriptine as the treatment for neuroleptic malignant syndrome; evidence is limited and supportive care and benzodiazepines are first-line, with these drugs reserved for moderate to severe cases.
