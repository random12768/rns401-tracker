Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Block-stacking rule (age x 3: 6 blocks at 2 yr, 9 at 3 yr) conflicts with the toddler/preschool table, which lists 4-6 blocks at 3-4 yr.
  • Red-flag cut-offs conflict within the guide: no babbling by 9 months (table) vs 12 months (ASD box); no 2-word phrases by 30 months (table) vs 24 months (ASD box). Current standard cut-offs are no babbling by 12 months and no 2-word phrases by 24 months.
  • The guide gives Moro reflex disappearance at 3-4 months and Babinski at 12-24 months; standard sources list Moro by about 3-6 months and Babinski by about 12 months, so these are approximate.
