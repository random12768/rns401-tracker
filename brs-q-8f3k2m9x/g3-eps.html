Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide frames EPS strictly by timeline; in practice stages overlap (akathisia can appear within days, and tardive dyskinesia can appear as early as about 3 months, or 1 month in older adults).
  • The guide lists propranolol as the only first-line akathisia drug; benzodiazepines and mirtazapine are common alternatives, and dose reduction should come first when feasible.
