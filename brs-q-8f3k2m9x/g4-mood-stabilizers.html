Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide says lithium causes both hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism and lists leukocytosis and acne; these are true but hypothyroidism is by far the main thyroid effect, and the guide's 'avoid' lists omit that loop diuretics have a smaller effect than thiazides.
  • The guide notes valproate has a 'faster onset than lithium'; this is accepted, but the hyperammonemia and thrombocytopenia effects are not covered in the table and are added here from standard sources.
  • The guide recommends halving lamotrigine with valproate; standard labeling reduces the starting dose and slows titration (about half the usual schedule) rather than a simple halving of an established dose.
