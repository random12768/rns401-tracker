Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide states ECT has 'no absolute contraindications (per FA 2026)'. This is accurate, but relative high-risk conditions (raised intracranial pressure/intracranial mass, recent MI, unstable cardiac disease, recent stroke) should be stated explicitly since the topic scope asks for them.
  • Guide says amnesia 'usually resolves within 6 months'. Anterograde deficits usually recover fast, but retrograde loss for events near the treatment course can be permanent in some patients; bilateral placement causes more cognitive effects than right unilateral.
