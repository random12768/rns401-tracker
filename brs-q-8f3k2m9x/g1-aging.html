Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide states sleep in aging shows "decreased REM and slow-wave sleep"; the consistent finding is a marked decrease in slow-wave (N3) sleep with increased awakenings and reduced REM latency/percentage only modestly, so REM is relatively preserved. The question tests slow-wave sleep.
  • The guide lists "increased MAO" and "decreased GABA" as consequences of aging; MAO-B increases with age, but the decreases in monoamines and GABA are less well established than the ACh and DA changes, so they should be treated as general trends.
  • The guide describes Down syndrome as the most common genetic cause of dementia; it is accurate for early-onset Alzheimer disease, but APOE e4 is the main genetic risk factor for late-onset disease.
