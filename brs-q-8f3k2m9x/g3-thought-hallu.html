Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide states 'Auditory hallucinations = schizophrenia until proven otherwise'; auditory hallucinations are also common in mood disorders with psychotic features, substance use, and some medical conditions, so this overstates specificity.
  • Guide lists 'Visual > auditory -> think medical/substance cause' as a pearl; this is a useful heuristic but visual hallucinations do occur in primary psychosis, so it is probabilistic rather than diagnostic.
  • Guide attributes cocaine formication to 'withdrawal'; cocaine bugs typically occur during intoxication or binge use rather than withdrawal.
