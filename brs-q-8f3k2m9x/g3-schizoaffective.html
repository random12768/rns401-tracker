Schizoaffective disorder vs mood disorder with psychotic features, and the duration timeline

Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide describes schizoaffective disorder as requiring a major mood episode 'PLUS' psychosis; DSM-5-TR also requires that mood symptoms be present for the majority of the total illness duration, which the guide mentions only loosely ('present for majority of illness').
  • Guide says about 2/3 of schizophreniform disorder progress to schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder and 1/3 recover; this is the usual Step 1 teaching, though reported progression rates vary by study (roughly 60 to 80%).
  • Guide lists 'functional decline REQUIRED' for schizophrenia as a separate criterion; DSM-5-TR criterion B (social/occupational dysfunction) is required, but it is a criterion rather than a separate feature, and the guide wording is a simplification.
