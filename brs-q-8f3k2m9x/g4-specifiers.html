Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide calls mood reactivity 'pathognomonic' for atypical features; it is characteristic and required for the specifier but not pathognomonic, and can be seen in other depressions.
  • The guide lists SSRIs as first line and MAOIs as 'superior' for atypical depression; current evidence shows MAOIs outperform tricyclics, with no clear superiority over SSRIs, so MAOIs are reserved for non-response.
  • The seasonal pattern box says to treat with SSRIs plus phototherapy; light therapy alone is also first line for mild to moderate seasonal depression.
