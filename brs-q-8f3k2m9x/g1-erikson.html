Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Stage ages leave a gap between stage 5 (12-18) and stage 6 (20-40); standard sources use about 12-18 and 19-40.
  • The guide lists 'OCD traits from strict toilet training' as an Erikson stage 2 outcome; this is a Freudian (anal) concept, and Erikson's failure outcome is shame and doubt.
