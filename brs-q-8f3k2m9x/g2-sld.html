Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide says IQ must be >=70 to diagnose SLD; DSM-5-TR dropped the IQ-discrepancy requirement (SLD can occur in gifted children and with lower IQ) and requires academic skills substantially below expectation for age for >=6 months despite targeted intervention.
  • Guide treats 'dyslexia/dysgraphia/dyscalculia' as DSM specifiers; DSM-5-TR uses 'with impairment in reading/written expression/mathematics' and treats these names as alternative descriptive terms.
