Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide says finality of death is understood by about age 7 in one section and places 5-9 years as "beginning to understand finality" in another; most sources describe a gradual transition, with irreversibility by 5-7 years and universality by 9-11 years.
  • The guide's table groups <5 years as one stage; infants (separation distress) and toddlers (reversible, like sleep) differ and the guide has no hospitalization-reaction table, so those details are from standard references.
  • The guide's age band for adolescents (10+) overlaps with the 9-11 range in which universality emerges; ages are approximate.
