Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • The guide's TORCH list includes HIV; classic TORCH is Toxoplasma, Other (syphilis, varicella, parvovirus B19, Zika, HIV), Rubella, CMV, Herpes simplex. The guide's version (Toxoplasma, Rubella, CMV, Herpes, Syphilis) is a simplification and is acceptable.
  • The guide lists isotretinoin effects as "craniofacial/cardiac"; it also causes thymic, parathyroid, and CNS defects, which is what the question uses.
  • The guide calls folic acid the way to prevent NTDs; women on valproate or with a prior NTD child are recommended higher doses (4 mg/day), not the standard 0.4 mg.
