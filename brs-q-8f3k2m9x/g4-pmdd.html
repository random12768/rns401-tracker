Notes on the study guide for this topic

Places where the guide is simplified, inconsistent or out of date. Use the standard textbook version for the exam where they differ.

  • Guide lists PMDD treatments but does not explain that SSRI benefit in PMDD begins within days (unlike 4-6 weeks in MDD), which is what makes luteal-phase-only dosing possible.
  • Guide gives the cyclothymia criterion as 2 years only; in children and adolescents it is 1 year (as for persistent depressive disorder).
